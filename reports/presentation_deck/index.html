The Single-Target Bottleneck in Modern Pharmacology

Traditional drug discovery relies on the "one-drug, one-target, one-disease" paradigm. However, clinical attrition remains over 90% because complex disorders emerge from robust, redundant network states that rapidly rewire around single molecular blocks.

Key Scientific Foundations & Conceptual Anchors

  • Barabási et al. (2011 Nature Reviews Genetics): Established that the human interactome is scale-free and that disease-causing mutations cluster into topologically cohesive disease modules.

  • Menche et al. (2015 Science): Derived the interactome module separation metric sAB, establishing that distinct diseases and drug targets occupy separated topological spheres.
  • Hamilton et al. (2017 NeurIPS): GraphSAGE inductive representation learning enables neighborhood aggregation on unseen interactome topologies without transductive leakage.

Clinical Mechanism: Overcoming CML Resistance
